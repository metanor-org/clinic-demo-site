How it works

From a flyer to the front desk, without the back-and-forth.

  1. Scan

    A QR code on a flyer or ad opens the clinic's chat.

  2. Ask

    Patients get fees, hours and directions in their language.

  3. Book

    They choose a department and time in a few taps.

  4. Get reminded

    A reminder lets them confirm, change or cancel.

  5. Arrive

    They show their token at reception, and staff know who's coming.
