Where do clinics lose patients?

Four everyday problems, each with a practical fix.

  • Messages left unanswered

    The assistant replies instantly about timings, services and location, and takes bookings at any hour.

  • Long waits at the front desk

    Patients receive a booking token on WhatsApp and arrive at the right time.

  • Patients who can't read the website

    Every page is available in Bengali, English and Arabic, switched with one tap.

  • Reminders that never go out

    The campaign view sends health reminders and clinic announcements to the right patients.
