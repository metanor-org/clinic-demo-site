Your staff stay in charge

The routine goes to the assistant. Decisions stay with your team.

The assistant handles

  • Prices, hours, location and offers your clinic approves
  • Pointing patients to the right department
  • Bookings, token numbers and reminders
  • Changes and cancellations

Your team handles

  • Medical questions and advice
  • Possible emergencies, with an instant alert
  • Insurance and special requests
  • Anything the assistant isn’t sure about

Ask a question

Does the assistant give medical advice?

No. It suggests a department to book and tells patients when to get urgent help. Medicine questions go to your staff.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Arabic and Bengali, in the chat and on the website. We add other languages your patients speak during setup.

Is the website really included?

Yes. Every package includes a clinic website built from the template, with your services, doctors, prices and branding.

Whose details does the assistant use?

Your clinic’s own services, doctors, hours, fees and address. We set them up with you before launch.

Are the demos linked to real patients?

No. Both demos run on made-up data and connect to no clinic, WhatsApp account or patient record.
